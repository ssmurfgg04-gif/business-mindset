## Anti-Pattern Check

Before extending a sprint past Day 14:
- [ ] Is the hypothesis poorly defined? (If yes, kill and redefine, don't extend)
- [ ] Is the experiment not generating signal? (If yes, redesign, don't extend)
- [ ] Am I rationalizing to avoid abandoning? (If yes, kill immediately)

**Rule**: Ambiguous Day 14 = kill. Redefine hypothesis. Run new sprint.
