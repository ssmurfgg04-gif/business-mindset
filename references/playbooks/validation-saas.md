# Validation Playbook: SaaS Opportunity

## Pre-Flight Checklist
- [ ] Landing page builder account (Carrd/Framer)
- [ ] Stripe account (test mode)
- [ ] Domain purchased ($10–15)
- [ ] Email sender configured (Mailgun/Resend)

## 7-Day Validation Sprint

### Day 1: Landing Page + Waitlist
- **Tool**: Carrd or Framer
- **Must Include**: Problem statement, solution mockup, waitlist email capture, price anchor ($X/mo).
- **Time**: 2–3 hours.
- **Kill Criteria**: No page live by end of Day 1.

### Day 2–3: Outreach to 50 Prospects
- **Tool**: Manual Gmail or outreach automation.
- **Target**: Specific job titles in specific industries.
- **Success Criteria**: ≥5 positive replies.
- **Kill Criteria**: <2 positive replies by Day 3.

### Day 4–5: Demo Calls (if ≥5 replies)
- **Tool**: Calendly.
- **Goal**: 3 demo calls verifying willingness to pay.
- **Success Criteria**: ≥1 verbal commitment to pay.
- **Kill Criteria**: Zero commitments after 3 calls.

### Day 6–7: Pre-Sale or Waitlist Depth
- **Tool**: Stripe payment link.
- **Goal**: 3 pre-sales OR 20 qualified waitlist signups.
- **Kill Criteria**: <3 pre-sales AND <20 signups.

## Go/No-Go Decision Matrix
| Metric | PASS | FLAG | REJECT |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-sales | ≥3 | 1–2 | 0 |
| Waitlist (qualified) | ≥20 | 10–19 | <10 |
| Demo commitment rate | ≥30% | 10–30% | <10% |
