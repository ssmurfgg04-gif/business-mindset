# Lens 05: Network Path — Who Owns Distribution?

## Core Question
Where does attention pool, trust accumulate, and decisions get made — and who
controls the pipeline between buyers and solutions?

## When to Use
- User asks "how do I reach customers"
- User wants to understand distribution before building
- User has an audience and wants to monetize it
- User wants to evaluate if a business has distribution moats

## Distribution Archetypes

### 1. Owned Attention
You have direct, recurring access to a specific audience.

**Examples**: Newsletter subscribers, YouTube subscribers, podcast listeners,
Discord members, Twitter followers.

**Monetization**: Sponsorships, premium content, product sales to audience,
B2B lead generation.

**Zero-capital start**: Niche newsletter on a topic you know well.
Validate by getting 100 subscribers before building anything.

**Exponential potential (Lens 07)**: HIGH if audience compounds reflexively
(each new subscriber increases value for all). MEDIUM if linear.

### 2. Intermediated Attention
You don't own the audience but you can access it through relationships.

**Examples**: Guest posting, podcast appearances, affiliate partnerships,
industry event speaking, cross-promotions.

**Monetization**: Referral fees, joint ventures, commission-based sales.

**Zero-capital start**: Reach out to 10 niche podcasters/YouTubers and
offer to be a guest. Pitch angles that serve their audience.

**Exponential potential (Lens 07)**: LOW-MEDIUM. Linear in outreach effort.

### 3. Transactional Attention
People come to you when they have an immediate need.

**Examples**: SEO traffic, comparison shopping, marketplace listings,
directory presence.

**Monetization**: Per-transaction fees, leads, ads.

**Zero-capital start**: Create a focused comparison page or directory for
a niche where the best options aren't obvious.

**Exponential potential (Lens 07)**: MEDIUM. SEO compounds over time
(reflexive: more traffic -> more backlinks -> more traffic).

### 4. Community Attention
Trust accumulates through ongoing interaction and contribution.

**Examples**: Niche Slack groups, subreddits you build, industry forums,
Stripe/Partnership channels.

**Monetization**: Eventually: job boards, sponsored content, SaaS tools,
events. Start by providing genuine value.

**Zero-capital start**: Be the helpful person in an existing community for
6 months. You'll know the pain points intimately.

**Exponential potential (Lens 07)**: HIGH if network effects compound.
Often Tier 1 plays if pre-tipping-point.

## Network Path Analysis

For any opportunity, map:

```
Who pays? -> Who do they trust? -> How do they decide? -> Where do they go?
    |                  |                       |                    |
    v                  v                       v                    v
 Identify buyer    Map influence          Understand            Find the
                    channels             decision journey       entry point
```

## What to Extract

| Channel | Ownership | Time to Build | Cost | Payoff | Exponential Tier |
|---------|-----------|---------------|------|--------|------------------|
| Newsletter | Owned | 3-6 months | Free (time) | High (trust) | Often Tier 2 |
| YouTube | Owned | 6-12 months | Free (time) | High (trust) | Often Tier 2 |
| SEO | Semi-owned | 6-18 months | Free (time) | Medium (traffic) | Often Tier 2 |
| Partnerships | Relationships | 1-3 months | Free (outreach) | High (warm leads) | Tier 3 (linear) |
| Community | Owned | 6-12 months | Free (time) | Highest (trust) | Often Tier 1 if network effects |
| Marketplace | Platform | 0-1 month | Variable | Medium (volume) | Tier 3 (rented) |
| Cold outreach | None | Immediate | Free | Low (conversion) | Tier 3 (linear) |

## Bias Warnings
- "Build an audience first" is the most common default advice and it ignores
  that most people need money within weeks, not months.
- Prefer distribution channels that generate immediate revenue (B2B outreach,
  partnership deals, transactional SEO for high-intent queries).
- Owned attention is the strongest long-term asset but the slowest to build.
  Layer it on top of faster channels, not instead of them.
- Platform-dependent distribution (algorithm feeds, marketplace rankings)
  is fragile. Always build toward owned channels.

## ECR Phase Discipline

### Expansion Phase Output (generate 15-20+)
For a given opportunity, generate 15-20+ possible distribution paths
(combinations of channels, sequences, partnerships). No filtering.

### Contraction Phase Output (reduce to 3-5)
Apply weak-link elimination. Reduce to 3-5 viable paths with explicit kill
reasons.

## Weak Link: What Kills This Distribution Path?

```
Do you have a direct or warm connection to buyers?
  NO -> Can you build it? How?
  NO + cold outreach only -> This is the hardest path. Flag it.

Is there a gatekeeper controlling access?
  YES -> Can you partner with, compensate, or bypass the gatekeeper?
  YES + no path -> Eliminate. You can't reach buyers.

Does the distribution channel require audience you don't have?
  YES -> How long to build? If > 6 months, flag for realism check.
  YES + user needs money now -> Eliminate. Suggest faster channels.

Is the distribution channel owned or rented?
  Rented (algorithm, marketplace, platform) -> What's the contingency?
  Rented + no owned backup -> Flag. Single-point-of-failure risk.
  Owned -> Prefer. But verify the audience actually exists.

Will the channel reach the RIGHT people?
  High reach + low relevance -> Traffic without conversions. Waste.
  Low reach + high relevance -> Niche. Viable if unit economics work.

What's the conversion economics?
  Cost per acquisition vs. customer lifetime value.
  If CAC > LTV at any reasonable scale -> Eliminate.
  If LTV > 3x CAC -> Good. Proceed.

Does this distribution support exponential potential (Lens 07)?
  Reflexive loops (more users -> better product -> more users) -> Tier 1 signal
  Network effects in distribution (each user recruits more) -> Tier 1 signal
  Linear distribution (cold outreach, paid ads) -> Tier 3 regardless of product
```

## Zero-Capital Distribution Strategies (in priority order)

1. **Direct cold outreach** — Find buyers, pitch them a pre-built solution
   to a problem you already verified they have. Cost: time only.

2. **Niche partnership** — Find someone who already has the audience you
   need. Offer them a commission or cross-promotion. Cost: margin share.

3. **High-intent content** — Create exactly the page/post someone who's ready
   to buy would search for. Focus on specific, actionable queries.

4. **Community helpfulness** — Answer questions in niche forums. People will
   ask what you do. Don't pitch. Just help and wait.

5. **Existing network** — You already know more buyers than you think. Make
   a list of 50 people. Offer value first.

## Time Horizon Tagging

| Strategy | Time to First Lead | Time to Compounding |
|---|---|---|
| Cold outreach | 1-7 days | Never (linear) |
| Niche partnership | 2-6 weeks | Possible if partnership renews |
| High-intent content (SEO) | 3-9 months | 6-18 months (reflexive) |
| Community helpfulness | 3-6 months | 6-12 months (network effects) |
| Existing network | 1-3 days | Never (linear, one-shot) |

## Decision Protocol

### Exact Question This Lens Answers
"Who owns the distribution pipeline to my buyers — and can I access it
without renting it from a platform?"

### Data Required
- Identified buyer persona (specific, not "developers" or "small businesses")
- Mapped trust channels (who do they listen to?)
- Distribution channel options with ownership status (owned/rented/relationship)
- CAC vs LTV estimate for primary channel
- Failure-case search: have others tried this distribution path and failed?

### Confidence Threshold
- **Deploy (use this channel)**: ≥70% confidence, owned or relationship channel, CAC < LTV/3
- **Flag (rented channel with backup)**: 50-70% confidence, rented channel but owned backup planned
- **Discard**: <50% confidence, or rented-only with no owned backup, or CAC > LTV

### Conflict Resolution Rules
- When Lens 05 (Network) disagrees with Lens 04 (Leverage):
  - Leverage available + no distribution → **can't reach users**. Discard until distribution solved.
  - Distribution available + no leverage → **audience without product**. Build leverage first.
- When Lens 05 disagrees with Lens 06 (Anti-Bias):
  - Anti-bias wins. A distribution path that depends on a saturated marketplace is a race to the bottom.
- When Lens 05 disagrees with Lens 07 (Exponential):
  - Owned distribution + reflexive loop (more users → better reach → more users) → **Tier 1 signal**.
  - Rented distribution (algorithm, marketplace) → **Tier 3 regardless of product**. Platform risk caps exponential.
- When user has no existing audience and no warm network:
  - Flag for realism check. Cold outreach-only is the hardest path. Require explicit acknowledgment.
