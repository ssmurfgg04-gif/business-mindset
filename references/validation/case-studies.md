# Validated Opportunity Case Studies & Base Rates

## Purpose
Reference database of real-world scored opportunities to calibrate current analyses against historical base rates.

## Case 1: Generic Freelance Logo Design on Fiverr
- **Market Assumption**: "I can make side-income designing logos on Fiverr."
- **Reality Check**: Over 4M active sellers; average new seller earns <$100 in first 6 months.
- **Asymmetric Score**: C=0, R=0, S=0, O=1, A=0, F=2 → 0/32.
- **Verdict**: REJECT for generic design. PASS only for hyper-niche specialized verticals (e.g. medical 3D rendering).

## Case 2: Upwork AI Automation Engineering
- **Market Assumption**: "Upwork is saturated, but AI workflow automation is new and growing."
- **Reality Check**: High platform competition, but specialized AI agent integration (n8n/Make + LLMs) commands $75–$230/hr with high retainer conversion.
- **Asymmetric Score**: C=2, R=1, S=1, O=2, A=1, F=1 → 8/32 (Borderline / FLAG).
- **Verdict**: FLAG — viable only with narrow vertical focus (e.g. automated lead routing for dental/real-estate offices).

## Case 3: Niche B2B Operational Newsletter → SaaS
- **Market Assumption**: "Build an audience first, monetize later."
- **Reality Check**: General audience newsletters have low conversion; B2B vertical newsletters maintain 35%+ open rates and strong sponsorship/SaaS upsell metrics.
- **Asymmetric Score**: C=2, R=2, S=1, O=2, A=2, F=0 → 16/32.
- **Verdict**: PASS — with 6-month runway and verified B2B pain point.
