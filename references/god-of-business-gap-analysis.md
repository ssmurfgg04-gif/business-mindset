# God of Business Gap Analysis — Comparing the Skill to the Ideal Operator

## Purpose

<purpose>
If a "god of business" existed — combining the greatest operators in history
(Buffett, Munger, Jobs, Musk, Bezos, Thiel, Walton, Grove, Christensen, Naval,
Drucker, Rockefeller) — what would they do that the current skill doesn't?

This document identifies the gaps between the skill (v0.5.3) and the ideal
operator, then closes them. The goal: make the skill operate as close to the
top 1% of operators as a cognition engine can.
</purpose>

<composite>

## Gap Analysis: Where the Skill Falls Short

<gaps>

### Gap 1: No Intuition / Pattern Recognition (CLOSED v0.6.0)
**God**: Jobs, Musk, Buffett all make major decisions on intuition that looks irrational.
**Skill (v0.5.3)**: Hyper-rational. No mechanism for gut feeling.
**Status**: **CLOSED** — Added Lens 17 (Intuition & Pattern Recognition). Distinguishes
expert intuition (calibrated, 10,000+ hours) from amateur intuition (bias). 5 intuition
signals, gut-analysis integration framework, calibration tracking.

### Gap 2: No Contrarian / Consensus Framework (CLOSED v0.6.0)
**God**: Thiel's "secrets," Buffett's "be fearful when others are greedy."
**Skill (v0.5.3)**: Treats consensus as a signal but doesn't systematically map it
or stress-test contrarian views.
**Status**: **CLOSED** — Added Lens 18 (Market Consensus & Contrarian Positioning).
3 layers of consensus (public/professional/capital), "Am I Right?" stress test,
contrarian classification (insight/ignorance/ego/timing), Thiel's secret framework.

### Gap 3: No Product Taste / Aesthetic Judgment
**God**: Jobs's ability to know what's good. "Real artists ship." Aesthetic sense.
**Skill**: Evaluates features but not product taste. Can't assess "is this beautiful?"
**Status**: **OPEN** — This is genuinely hard for an AI. Partial mitigation: ask the
user "does this feel elegant?" and treat their answer as a signal. Add to Lens 17
(intuition) as a type of expert intuition.

### Gap 4: No Narrative / Storytelling Capability
**God**: Jobs (launch keynotes), Musk (mission narrative), Bezos (shareholder letters).
All masters at crafting narratives that attract talent, capital, customers.
**Skill**: Has output format but no narrative lens. Can't assess "is the story compelling?"
**Status**: **OPEN** — Could add a Lens 19 (Narrative & Positioning) but narrative is
hard to evaluate structurally. Partial mitigation: include "narrative check" in
output — "Can you tell this story in one sentence that makes someone say 'tell me more'?"

### Gap 5: No People Judgment / Reading People
**God**: Buffett ("we look for three things: intelligence, energy, and integrity"),
Musk (reading engineers), Bezos (hiring bar raisers).
**Skill**: Has hiring protocol but not "reading people" intuition.
**Status**: **OPEN** — People judgment is largely intuitive (Lens 17 covers partially).
Add to Lens 17 as a specific intuition signal. Also: trust the user's people judgment
when evaluating hires/partners.

### Gap 6: No Political / Regulatory Navigation
**God**: Musk (navigating SpaceX/Tesla regulations), Grove (navigating Intel politics),
Bezos (navigating antitrust).
**Skill**: Treats regulation as a barrier (Lens 12), not a game to play.
**Status**: **PARTIALLY CLOSED** — Add "regulatory navigation" to Lens 10 (Competitor
Teardown) as a dimension. The skill can't play politics, but can advise on it.

### Gap 7: No Second/Third-Order Thinking (PARTIALLY CLOSED)
**God**: Munger ("and then what?"), Howard Marks (second-level thinking).
**Skill**: Has systems thinking (feedback loops) but not explicit "and then what?" analysis.
**Status**: **PARTIALLY CLOSED** — Add to pre-mortem (Lens 06) as explicit second-order
analysis: "If we do X, then Y happens. If Y happens, then Z. Is Z acceptable?"

### Gap 8: No Asymmetric Conviction Framework
**God**: Thiel's "secrets" + courage to act. Musk's "I'm going to Mars." Conviction
that overrides skepticism.
**Skill**: Skeptical by design (80% rejection). No framework for "I KNOW this and I'm
going to act regardless of what analysis says."
**Status**: **PARTIALLY CLOSED** — Lens 17 (Intuition) + Lens 18 (Contrarian) together
provide this: if you have expert intuition AND contrarian-from-insight, you have
asymmetric conviction. The skill still requires verification, but recognizes that
conviction is a legitimate override signal.

### Gap 9: No Founder-Market Fit Beyond Skills
**God**: Naval's "specific knowledge" + obsession. The "I can't stop thinking about
this" signal.
**Skill**: Has "fit" as one axis in Lens 11 scoring, but doesn't weight obsession.
**Status**: **PARTIALLY CLOSED** — Add "obsession" as a 6th scoring axis in Lens 11.
If the user can't stop thinking about the opportunity, that's a signal (positive or
negative — obsession can also be bias).

### Gap 10: No Time Horizon Flexibility
**God**: Buffett/Bezos think in decades. Musk compresses timelines. Thiel thinks in
definite futures.
**Skill**: Has time-horizon tagging but doesn't adapt strategy to time horizon.
**Status**: **OPEN** — Add time-horizon lens: "Is this a 10-year play or a 2-year play?"
Different strategies for different horizons.

### Gap 11: No Capital Allocation as Primary Job
**God**: Buffett/Bezos treat capital allocation as the CEO's #1 job.
**Skill**: Has Lens 15 (Capital Allocation) but it's one lens among 18, not the primary
frame.
**Status**: **PARTIALLY CLOSED** — Lens 15 exists and is strong. The gap is emphasis:
capital allocation should be the final question for any opportunity, not one of many.

### Gap 12: No "Day 1" Mentality
**God**: Bezos's Day 1 vs Day 2. Day 1 = customer obsession, high-velocity decisions,
embracing external trends. Day 2 = stasis, irrelevance, death.
**Skill**: Doesn't assess "is this a Day 1 or Day 2 business?"
**Status**: **OPEN** — Add Day 1/Day 2 assessment to Lens 13 (Growth).

</gaps>

## The Gap-Closing Improvements (v0.6.0)

<improvements>

### Improvement 1: Lens 17 — Intuition (NEW, closes Gap 1)
Full intuition framework: expert vs amateur, 5 signals, calibration tracking,
gut-analysis integration. See `references/lenses/17-intuition.md`.

### Improvement 2: Lens 18 — Market Consensus & Contrarian (NEW, closes Gap 2)
3-layer consensus mapping, "Am I Right?" stress test, contrarian classification,
Thiel secrets. See `references/lenses/18-market-consensus.md`.

### Improvement 3: Obsession as scoring axis (Lens 11 update, closes Gap 9)
Add obsession as 6th axis in Lens 11 (Opportunity Sifting):
- 0: No particular interest
- 1: Mild interest
- 2: "I can't stop thinking about this" (obsession signal)

Obsession is a double-edged signal: it can be genuine founder-market fit OR
sunk-cost bias. The skill flags it but doesn't auto-promote.

### Improvement 4: Second-order thinking in pre-mortem (closes Gap 7)
Update Lens 06 pre-mortem to require explicit second-order analysis:
- "If we do X, then Y. If Y, then Z. Is Z acceptable?"
- Most pre-mortems stop at first-order failure. The god operator thinks to
  second and third order.

### Improvement 5: Capital allocation as final gate (closes Gap 11)
Update pipeline so Lens 15 (Capital Allocation) runs as the FINAL gate before
verdict, not as one of many lenses. Every PASS must answer: "Is this the
highest-ROI use of the operator's capital and time?"

### Improvement 6: Narrative check in output (addresses Gap 4)
Add to output format: "Can you tell this opportunity's story in one sentence
that makes someone say 'tell me more'?" If no, the narrative is weak — flag
it.

</improvements>

## The Remaining Gaps (Honest Acknowledgment)

<remaining>
Some gaps can't be fully closed by a cognition engine:

### Product taste (Gap 3)
AI can't reliably assess "is this beautiful?" The skill can ask the user and
treat their answer as a signal, but can't develop taste itself. This remains
a human competency.

### People judgment (Gap 5)
Reading people in interviews, partnerships, hires is largely intuitive. The
skill provides structure (hiring protocol) but can't read body language or
sense character. Human competency.

### Political navigation (Gap 6)
Politics requires relationships, reputation, context that AI lacks. The skill
can advise on regulatory landscape but can't navigate office politics or
personal relationships. Human competency.

### Day 1 mentality (Gap 12)
This is cultural, not analytical. The skill can ask "is this Day 1 or Day 2?"
but maintaining Day 1 culture is an ongoing human practice. Human competency.

**The honest assessment**: The skill can be 80% of the god of business.
The remaining 20% — taste, people, politics, culture — requires the human
operator. The skill's job is to make the human a better operator, not to
replace them.
</remaining>

## The Meta-Comparison

<meta>
**The god of business** would use this skill as a tool, not a replacement.
They would:
- Run the analysis (skill provides)
- Apply intuition (Lens 17, but the god's intuition is better than the skill's)
- Stress-test contrarian views (Lens 18, but the god has more practice)
- Make the final call themselves (skill advises, doesn't decide)

**The skill's role**: not to BE the god of business, but to make the operator
more god-like. The skill handles the analysis, verification, and skepticism
that humans are bad at. The human handles the taste, people, politics, and
conviction that AI is bad at.

**The partnership**:
- Skill: 16 lenses + verification + skepticism + calibration
- Human: intuition + taste + people + politics + conviction
- Together: closer to the god of business than either alone

This is the honest positioning. The skill isn't a replacement for greatness.
It's a tool that makes greatness more likely by handling the parts humans
are bad at (systematic verification, anti-bias, calibration tracking).
</meta>
