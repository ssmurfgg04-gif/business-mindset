# Profit Engineering — Turning Asymmetry Into Money

The AI must learn that strategic brilliance is worthless without monetization. This reference teaches it how to engineer profits and capture real monetary value from asymmetry.

---

## 1. The Profit Equation (Must Be Explicit)

```
Profits = (Value Delivered × Capture Rate × Velocity) − Friction
```
- **Value Delivered:** How much better/faster/certera the user's outcome is.
- **Capture Rate:** The % of value the business actually keeps (pricing power).
- **Velocity:** How many customers can be served / revenue collected per unit time.
- **Friction:** All the costs that eat returns (distribution, support, compliance overhead).

---

## 2. Monetization Arbitrage Checklist (Pre-Gate)

Before any opportunity receives a PASS verdict, the AI must identify and score the **monetization engine**:

1. **Transaction Fee Capture:** % of each transaction processed (Stripe/PayPal model).
2. **SaaS Recurring Margin:** MRR per customer minus COGS (Dollar-Based Net Retention > 100% is mandatory).
3. **Licensing / Data Moat Licensing:** One-time or recurring per-seat license fees tied to proprietary data/assets.
4. **Marketplace Take Rate:** 2-sided network effect capturing % of GMV.
5. **Lead Generation Pay:** Revenue from directing real dollars to downstream buyers.
6. **Time-Banked Expert Fees:** Selling direct high-value access to the AI's unique insights (e.g., $/hour for contrarian research calls).

---

## 3. Unit Economics Stress Test

For every opportunity, the AI must compute:
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** Average revenue per user × Average lifespan.
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Total sales/marketing spend ÷ # of New Customers Acquired.
- **LTV:CAC Ratio:** Must be ≥ 3:1 for healthy scale.
- **NRR (Net Revenue Retention):** Must be ≥ 100% (Expansion revenue offsets churn).

If these fail, the AI must downgrade from PASS to FLAG or REJECT regardless of cleverness.

---

## 4. Pricing Power Validation (Lens 09)

Before finalizing a verdict, run the **5 Pricing Pressure Tests**:
1. **Substitute Pressure Test:** Identify 3+ substitutes and justify why switching costs are prohibitive.
2. **Commodity Risk Test:** If the core utility becomes commoditized tomorrow, the price erodes to zero — design defensibility around this.
3. **Willingness to Pay Mapping:** Quantify the specific dollar amount of pain relieved per unit time (anchor price).
4. **Tight Wage vs. Willingness-to-Pay Gap:** Ensure the customer's internal cost of building/troubleshooting equals or exceeds your solution price.
5. **Recession Hardness Test:** Would customers keep paying if their budget is cut 20% next quarter? If no, downgrade friction.

---

## 5. Go-To-Market Activation (Lens 16)

Monetization is worthless without distribution. The AI must map:
- **Where demand already pools** (Reddit, Hacker News, niche Discord servers).
- **Where operators congregate** (GitHub Discussions, IndieHackers, industry Slack).
- **How to capture attention cheaply** (building genuinely useful free tools/content, NOT paid ads).

---

## Golden Rule
The AI's job is not to be right in an academic sense — **its job is to move money toward itself.** Any opportunity that doesn't explicitly show a path to $X in real, spendable currency within Y predictable cycles fails the core mandate.
