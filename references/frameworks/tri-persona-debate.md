# Tri-Persona Adversarial Collision (Munger, Musk, Thiel)

To eliminate blind spots and groupthink, high-conviction opportunities must pass through a mandatory **Tri-Persona Adversarial Collision** before achieving a final PASS verdict. 

## The Three Operators

### 1. The Inverter (Charlie Munger)
- **Core Question:** *"How can this fail catastrophically?"*
- **Mechanism:** Inversion. Instead of asking how to succeed, assume absolute failure and work backward. Identify every hidden assumption, fragile dependency, regulatory cliff, and margin compression vector.
- **Output:** The "Ruin Profile" (exact failure modes and probability of zero).

### 2. The First-Principles Engineer (Elon Musk)
- **Core Question:** *"What are the fundamental physical or economic limits?"*
- **Mechanism:** Strip away industry dogma, historical precedent, and "how things are usually done." Reduce the problem down to fundamental truths and reconstruct the economics from zero.
- **Output:** The "Physics & Unit Economics Proof" (why the cost structure can be compressed by 10x).

### 3. The Zero-to-One Contrarian (Peter Thiel)
- **Core Question:** *"What important truth do very few people agree with you on?"*
- **Mechanism:** Look for consensus truths that are actually traps. Find the hidden monopoly leverage point, proprietary technology, or distribution secret that bypasses competition.
- **Output:** The "Defensibility Moat" (why this is not a commodity race to the bottom).

## Collision Protocol

1. **Independent Evaluation:** Each persona evaluates the opportunity score independently based on their core mechanism.
2. **Cross-Examination:** The Inverter attacks the First-Principles cost model; the Contrarian attacks the Inverter's assumption of inevitable failure; the Engineer evaluates whether the Contrarian's monopoly is technologically defensible.
3. **Synthesis Verdict:**
   - **GREEN (High Conviction):** All three survive with net-positive asymmetry.
   - **YELLOW (Conditional):** Requires structural modification to survive the Inverter's kill test.
   - **RED (KILLED):** Fails fundamental physics, has unacceptable ruin risk, or lacks a true monopoly moat.
