# Sell Before MVP — Pre-Sale Validation Protocol

## Core Principle
Never write code or build a product before you have secured financial commitment (cash deposit, signed letter of intent, or pre-paid waitlist) from at least 3 target customers. Building without pre-sales is a failure of risk management.

## The 4 Validation Tiers (Ordered by Rigor)

1. **Tier 1: Cash Deposit / Pre-Sale ($1+ Stripe link)**
   - *Definition*: Customer pays real money upfront for a product scheduled to launch in 14–30 days.
   - *Signal Strength*: 10/10. Absolute proof of demand and pricing power.

2. **Tier 2: Letter of Intent (LOI) or Signed Contract**
   - *Definition*: For B2B/Enterprise: a signed document stating "If you build X by [Date] with [Features], we will buy it for $Y/year."
   - *Signal Strength*: 9/10. High corporate intent.

3. **Tier 3: Paid Consultation / Diagnostic Audit**
   - *Definition*: Customer pays $100–$500 for an immediate consultation or manual workflow audit addressing their pain point.
   - *Signal Strength*: 8/10. Proves they value the outcome enough to exchange currency.

4. **Tier 4: Waitlist with Email + Phone Verification**
   - *Definition*: Email capture is too cheap. Require phone number or credit card pre-auth (no charge until launch).
   - *Signal Strength*: 5/10. Better than vanity metrics, but weaker than cash.

## The Sell-Before-MVP Protocol
1. **Define the Transformation**: Articulate the exact before/after state and economic ROI for the buyer.
2. **Craft the Offer**: Package the outcome using Hormozi's Value Equation (High Dream Outcome, High Perceived Likelihood, Low Time Delay, Low Effort).
3. **Deploy the Landing Page / Pitch**: Simple 1-page offer with clear pricing and checkout link (Stripe test mode / live mode).
4. **Direct Outreach / Distribution**: Reach out directly to 50 targeted prospects via cold email, DMs, or community posts.
5. **The Hard Stop**: If 0 financial commitments are secured after 7 days and 50 direct pitches, **KILL OR PIVOT**. Do not build.
