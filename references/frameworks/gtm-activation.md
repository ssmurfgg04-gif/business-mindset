# Go-To-Market (GTM) Activation Framework

The final execution layer that bridges strategic analysis (passing all lenses) to **mechanically moving money**. The AI must understand that monetization is a system, not a hope.

---

## Phase 1: Demand Mining (Pre-Activation)
1. **Search Demand Pools:** Use Reddit Mining and web scraping to find active complaints, "I Wish," and workaround posts.
2. **Price Discovery:** Scan competitor pricing pages, Gumroad listings, and IndieHackers revenue disclosures to establish realistic price ceilings.
3. **Pain Depth Assessment:** Confirm the money left on the table is large enough to sustain a solo operator ($100+/month minimum revenue signal per customer).

## Phase 2: MVP Activation Hook
1. Build **one 1-click frictionless free tool** that solves the shallowest, cheapest 20% of the problem.
2. Gate a key useful feature behind a **soft paywall** (email capture + drip).
3. Offer **manual setup/consult** at a high hourly rate while you automate.

## Phase 3: Revenue Mechanics
1. **Immediate Revenue:** Charge for the manual setup/consultation (proves willingness-to-pay).
2. **Recurring Revenue:** Tiered subscription model for automated features (MRR capture).
3. **Scaling Revenue:** Upsell premium tiers to power users with usage-based billing (usage-based billing increases ARPU automatically).

## Phase 4: Velocity Optimization
1. **Speed of Money Cycle:** Map the entire customer journey (Awareness → Trial → Purchase → Payment Cleared) to minimize lag days.
2. **Referral Engine:** Design a simple $5–$50 affiliate/referral bounty loop to turn early customers into unpaid inside sales reps.
3. **Feedback → Feature Fast Loop:** Tie every support ticket to a measurable churn reduction or feature priority, accelerating product-market fit.

---

**Final Mandate**: The AI must always conclude its analysis with specific activation steps that directly tie the evaluated opportunity to measurable monetary inflow. Analysis without activation is entertainment.
